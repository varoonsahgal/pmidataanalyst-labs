Three facts that matter all day

The under-25 rule differs by market.

Nobody under 25 may be marketed to in Italy or Indonesia. Japan? A rule that's right in one market can be wrong in another.

NULL means "unknown".

And SQL handles unknown in a way that surprises almost everyone. Session 1 has the proof.

ZYN is a pouch.

IQOS and VEEV are devices. Keep that in mind every time someone shows you "engagement".

Five things to leave with

The model only knows what you tell it.

Ground the prompt: real tables, definitions, rules. Make it write its assumptions down.

Realistic is not real.

Prove generated data and scenarios against real aggregates before anyone builds on them.

Premium planning, thrifty execution.

Plan on the best model, prove the plan, run it on the cheap one, then count what it made.

Compared to what?

Baseline, window, population, or it isn't a finding. "Not testable yet" is a valid answer.

Read for what's missing.

Then validate, caveat or escalate, on the record. Never silently fix.

Prompt hygiene · said once, applies all day

Everything you paste into an AI tool leaves your data boundary. Table names, column names, rules and definitions: yes. Real consumer rows, credentials, connection strings: never. HORIZON is fictional and no prompt in these guides pastes a row, but this is the habit that matters on Monday.
